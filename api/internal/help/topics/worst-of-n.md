title: Worst of N
keywords: worst segments services items children caption roll up no company value overview

Some metrics are reported only below the scope you are looking at: by division, by team, or by service, with no single value for the company. The overview and the report still give such a metric a status at your scope: the **worst** status among the units or items under it that reported one. The caption says so, for example "worst of 3 segments" or "worst of 2 services".

- Only children that reported count. One with no data, or not applicable, is left out of N.
- "Worst" follows the status scale's ranks (Red is worse than Yellow).
- When the scope has its own value, that value and its status are shown and no caption appears.

Worst of N is a status, not a number: there is no combined value behind it. To see which child drives it, open the metric and look at the units or items under the scope.
