title: Restatement

A scorecard's methodology (its measures, metrics, formulas and status rules) is kept in **versions**. Each published version takes effect from a period. Every column of a scorecard is computed under the version in force at that column's last month, so a definition change applies from its effective month on and earlier months stay as they were measured.

**Restating** computes every column under one chosen version instead, whatever its status (published, superseded, in review or a draft). Use it to compare months like for like across a definition change, or to preview what a draft would do to past months.

When restating, measures derived from records are derived again with the chosen version's rules. Values typed or imported directly are kept as they are.

A restatement is a view only. Nothing stored changes, and the normal view and published reports are unaffected.

To see what changed between two versions, compare them with the methodology diff. To ask "would this metric have been red last year under the new rules?", look at the view restated to the new version for those periods.
