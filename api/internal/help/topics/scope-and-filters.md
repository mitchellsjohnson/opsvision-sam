**Scope** is the part of the organization a page shows: the whole company or one org unit, with the units and tracked items under it. A scorecard opens at your default scope, which you can save from the scope filters at the top of a scorecard.

**Access** is separate: a membership can be limited to some org units, some metrics, or both. You see a unit when it, or a unit above it, is in your scope, and you see only the metrics in your scope. Everything SAM reads goes through the same rules, so SAM never sees more than you can.

The org structure changes over time: units move, are added and retire. By default each period is shown under the structure of that period. Choosing the **current** structure shows every period as if today's organization had always existed, which helps compare a unit with its own past after a reorganization.

Views can be narrowed to some metrics and some units or items, and can show months, quarters, halves, fiscal years, and year-to-date columns.

When a number looks wrong for a scope, check which units roll into it for that period, whether a child has no data (it is skipped, not counted as zero), and whether the unit has its own entered value (which wins over the roll-up of its children).
