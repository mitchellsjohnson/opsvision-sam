Months are not locked. Every scorecard value is **live**: it is computed when you look, from the data, methodology and org structure stored now. A late import, a corrected entry or an override can change any month, including past ones.

Periods are fiscal: `2026-M10` is the tenth month of fiscal year 2026, and a fiscal year is named for the calendar year it ends in. Quarters (`Q1`–`Q4`), halves (`H1`, `H2`) and years (`FY`) are made of those months. Year-to-date columns run from the first fiscal month through the month named.

What does not change is a **published report**. Publishing a month freezes a copy of that month's company report as a numbered revision. Reading it later shows the numbers as published; if the live data has moved since, the report says the data was revised and lists the cells that changed. Publishing again creates the next revision; earlier revisions are kept.

So, to explain why a number differs from what was reported: compare the published revision with the live report for the same month, then look at the history of the cells that changed.

The record import preview has a section for changes waiting on locked months. It stays empty until months can be locked.
